About our school

Rockfield Schools is a school in Odogunyan, Ikorodu, where children learn in a safe, friendly place and are taught to do their best and tell the truth.

Our teachers work with parents to help each child learn well, behave well and grow in confidence.

Hardwork and Honesty

This is our motto and the way we teach. Pupils learn that success comes from steady effort and good character.
